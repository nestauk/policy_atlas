# Breakfast clubs in Westland (SYNTHETIC REPORT, no Key findings section)

**Question:** What is the effect of free school breakfast clubs on pupil attendance in primary schools in Westland?

This report is an invented example used to demonstrate the evaluator. It is not a real Policy Atlas output. It deliberately has no Key findings section.

## Overview

Free breakfast clubs are certain to raise attendance by around 10 percentage points in Westland primary schools, according to the trial evidence [1].

Reading outcomes were not examined in the studies we found. Several studies discuss breakfast provision in secondary schools, which is outside the question.

## Durability

Attendance was measured over one school year in every trial, and no trial followed pupils for more than 18 months, so whether the gain lasts is not known [4].

## Delivery

Some commentators suggest that who runs the club could matter for participation, although the picture is mixed and the studies are hard to interpret [5].
