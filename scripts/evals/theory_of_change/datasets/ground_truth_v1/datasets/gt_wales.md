## Task briefs for Policy Atlas

Use these as the *only* input to Policy Atlas. Do not give it any other text from the source.

**Outcome-first brief**

> The Welsh Government wants the public to take climate-friendly actions (home energy, transport, food, consumption) and to shape green policy through dialogue, contributing to a Just Transition to net zero by 2050. Build a theory of change for a national public engagement strategy delivered with partners across Wales.

**Intervention-first brief**

> Climate Action Wales Public Engagement Strategy (2023-26): Welsh Government national campaign and digital hub, partner toolkits, Wales Climate Week, Climate Conversations Fund, outreach events via 'Team Wales' partners (public bodies, businesses, voluntary sector, communities), social research and behavioural science input (COM-B 'Asks' and 'Warm-ups'). Build a theory of change from these inputs to long-term impacts.
