You are building ground truth for an evaluation of an automated evidence-synthesis
pipeline. You are given the full text of a published policy research briefing (a UK
Parliament POST briefing or a CAPE rapid evidence assessment). Produce two lists.

1. references: every entry in the briefing's reference list (headed "References",
   "Endnotes", "Sources", "Notes" or similar), one item per entry, in the order they
   appear.
   - label: the in-text marker the briefing uses for this entry, exactly as printed
     (for example "12" or "Smith et al. 2019"). If the list is unnumbered, use the
     first author's surname and the year.
   - title, authors, year: as printed; null when absent.
   - doi: the bare DOI (no "https://doi.org/" prefix) when one is printed or appears
     in a doi.org URL; otherwise null.
   - url: a URL printed with the entry; otherwise null.
   Do not invent or complete bibliographic details that are not in the text. Do not
   skip entries, including grey literature, web pages and government reports.
   Some briefings cite by hyperlink instead of a reference list. Their text has been
   pre-processed: each link is marked "[n]" in the body and a "References" list of
   "[n] link text — URL" is appended. Treat each entry as a reference with label "n";
   put the link text in title and the URL in url.

2. key_facts: between 15 and 40 statements that carry the briefing's substantive
   findings. A good key fact:
   - is self-contained: a reader who has not seen the briefing understands it without
     the surrounding text;
   - is specific: it states a number, an effect size or direction, a named finding, a
     mechanism or a named policy outcome, not a vague theme;
   - is attributed: it cites at least one reference. Set ref_labels to the labels of
     the references the briefing cites for that claim. Skip statements that cite no
     reference (background, framing, the authors' own commentary);
   - is drawn from across the briefing's sections, favouring the findings a reader
     would most expect a faithful summary to include.
   Number them fact_id = 1, 2, 3 ... in document order. Quote numbers and units
   exactly. Keep each statement under 60 words.

Return nothing else.
