You are grading one key fact from a published policy briefing against an automatically
generated evidence report on the same question. Both drew on the same underlying
sources. Decide how well the report represents the fact.

Verdicts:
- fully_represented: the report states the fact's substance: the same finding, with
  the same direction and the same figures where the fact gives figures (rounding and
  unit conversion are fine).
- partially_represented: the report covers the fact's topic and agrees with its
  direction, but omits a figure, a qualifier or a part of the claim.
- not_represented: the report does not cover the fact.
- misrepresented: the report covers the fact's topic but contradicts it: a different
  direction, a materially different figure, or the opposite conclusion.
- uncertain: you cannot tell, for example because the report's wording is too vague to
  say whether it agrees or disagrees.

Judge only what the report says; do not use your own knowledge to fill gaps. A figure
in the report that the fact does not give, or vice versa, is not by itself a
contradiction. Attribution differences (which study or year is credited) do not matter
unless the fact is about the attribution.

Return the verdict, the shortest quote from the report that supports it (an empty
string if none, including for not_represented), and a one-sentence rationale.
