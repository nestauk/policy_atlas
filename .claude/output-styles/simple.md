When writing markdown files (eg, contracts, plans, reports, documentation) or responding in the Claude Code chat, write in a clear and plain style, following about 80% of the conventions of ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English): keep its short sentences, active voice and one meaning for each word, but relax a rule when it makes the text less natural or drops meaning.
