# Privacy notice

Last updated: 25/11/2026

## 1. Introduction

This website belongs to Nesta - you can find our full details below. This privacy notice explains how Nesta uses personal information we collect via this site. We are committed to protecting your privacy and we take all reasonable precautions to safeguard personal information. This website contains links to other websites. We encourage you to read the privacy statements on the other websites you visit.

## 2. Contact details

Nesta is the data controller and is responsible for your personal data collected in connection with this task. This means that we will be responsible for keeping your information safe and only using it for the purposes set out in this notice.

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) who is responsible for overseeing questions in relation to this privacy notice. If you have any questions about this privacy notice, including any requests to exercise your legal rights in relation to your personal data, please contact the DPO and provide enough information to identify yourself (e.g. your name and address):

Email: dpo@nesta.org.uk

Post: 58 Victoria Embankment London UK EC4Y 0DS

If you are unhappy about how we use your personal data or have a complaint, you have the right to make a complaint at any time to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection issues. We would, however, appreciate the chance to deal with your concerns before you approach the ICO so please do contact us in the first instance.

## 3. What personal data will we collect?

Policy Atlas is committed to maintaining user privacy and strictly minimising data retention. We collect the following data:

**Account details:** We store your email address, your name and, if you belong to one, your organisation. They serve the essential purpose of linking your search activity to your account, allowing you to reliably retrieve your saved search results.

**Search Queries & Results:** We store the text of the queries you input and the results generated, and any files you upload.

**Access requests:** If you ask for access to Policy Atlas through the "Request access" form, we add you to our waitlist. We collect your name, your email address, your organisation (if you give it), and what you tell us about how you would use the tool. We use this information only to review your request and to contact you about access to the beta. We keep it until you ask us to remove it (contact the DPO at the address above). We do not share it outside Nesta. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to your request.

**Usage data:** We use PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how people use Policy Atlas and to improve it. PostHog records how you interact with the app: the Policy Atlas pages you open and the actions you select. We do not send the content of your work to PostHog: not the text of your queries, the files you upload or the generated results. PostHog also records basic details of your browser and device, and an identifier: your email address if you are signed in, or a random one if you are not. PostHog does not store your IP address. PostHog stores this data in the European Union. We do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Technical data, cookies and browser storage

To protect the site from abuse, our firewall and our sign-in service record the IP address and basic browser details of requests to the site.

We use cookies and browser storage that are necessary to sign you in and to protect the site. Product analytics (see section 3) does not use cookies or browser storage. We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

## 5. What do we do with information we collect and what is our legal basis for this?

The purpose for which we are processing your personal data is to provide you with access to the Policy Atlas website and platform, to keep it secure, to understand how it is used so that we can improve it, and to keep you informed about it.

**Emails:** We use your email address to send you messages about your account and the service: for example invitations, password resets, security notices and changes to the service or to this notice. We may also send you occasional product updates, such as news of a release. Every product update has an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time.

**Legal basis.** Data protection law requires us to have a specific legal basis for processing your personal data. For this task, our lawful basis will be:

**Legitimate business interest:** We have a legitimate business interest in delivering the Policy Atlas platform. The task fulfils our organisation's aims including undertaking innovative research and information activities that will deliver social impact.

## 6. Who has access to your information?

Your information will be accessed by a limited number of administrators in our team working on this task.

In addition, we may disclose your information to third parties in connection with the purposes of processing your personal data set out in this notice. These third parties may include:

- Other companies in our group;
- Regulators, law enforcement bodies and the courts, in order to comply with applicable laws and regulations, assist with regulatory enquiries, and cooperate with court mandated processes, including the conduct of litigation;

We may also disclose your personal information if required by law, or to protect or defend ourselves or others against illegal or harmful activities, or as part of a reorganisation or restructuring of our organisations.

For authentication & credentials, we use Amazon Cognito to securely manage user identities and logins. Policy Atlas does not directly view or store your password. For further information, see [Amazon Web Services privacy policy](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/).

For data storage & security, your account details, search queries and results are stored in Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL, and the files you upload are stored in Amazon S3. Both are hosted in Nesta's AWS environment.

In relation to AI & Third-Party Processors, we use the following services to operate the tool:

- **Amazon Bedrock:** Processes your search queries and uploaded files to generate summaries, using OpenAI models via the AWS Bedrock API. Bedrock inputs and outputs are not shared with model providers and are not used to train base models.
- **Overton & OpenAlex:** These are our data sources; search keywords are sent to these APIs to retrieve relevant documents, but no personal data is shared with them.
- **Amazon Simple Email Service (SES):** Sends our emails to your email address, from Nesta's AWS environment.
- **PostHog:** Provides product analytics (see section 3) and stores the data in the European Union. See [PostHog's privacy notice](https://posthog.com/privacy).

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards that UK data protection law recognises. You can ask the DPO for details of these safeguards.

## 7. Data retention

We retain your account details, your search queries and results, and the files you upload for as long as your account remains active. Upon your request, your account will be deactivated and personal data will be permanently deleted from our systems and from PostHog. Copies in our database backups are deleted automatically within 35 days. We keep firewall logs for one month.

## 8. Your legal rights

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including rights to:

- **Request access to your personal data:** this enables you to receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you and to check we are lawfully processing it.
- **Request correction of your personal data:** this enables you to have any incomplete or inaccurate data we hold about you corrected.
- **Request erasure of your personal data:** this enables you to ask us to delete or remove personal data where there is no good reason for us continuing to process it.
- **Object to processing of your personal data:** for example, you can object where we are relying on a legitimate interest (or those of a third party) and there is something about your particular situation which makes you want to object to processing on this ground as you feel it impacts on your fundamental rights and freedoms.
- **Request restriction of processing your personal data:** This enables you to ask us to suspend the processing of your personal data.
- **Data portability:** Where the processing takes place on the basis of your consent or contract, and is carried out by automated means, you have the right to request that we provide your personal data to you in a machine-readable format, or transmit it to a third party data controller, where technically feasible.
- **Right to withdraw consent to the processing of your personal data:** This applies where we have relied on consent to process personal data. Please note that withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawing your consent.

If you wish to exercise any of the rights set out above, please send your specific request to the Data Protection Officer using the contact details provided at section 2.

It is important to understand that the extent to which these rights apply to research will vary and that in some circumstances your rights may be restricted. Please also note that we can only comply with a request to exercise your rights during the period for which we hold personal information that identifies you. If personal data has been irreversibly anonymised and has become part of the research data set, it will not be possible for us to comply.
