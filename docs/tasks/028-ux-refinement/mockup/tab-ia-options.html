Pupil attendance in UK schools

What is the evidence from academic literature on the effects of school-based interventions on pupil attendance in UK school settings?

Key findings

  • School–home communication carries the strongest attendance-direction signal in the material read for this question. refs
  • Mentoring as a UK impact intervention has weaker citable evidence than school–home communication. refs
  • The clearest UK experimental result is a null: the Greater Manchester PATHS cluster trial reported no significant differences. refs
  • The evidence base is broad but thin for UK attendance effects — twelve documents screened in, four of them systematic reviews. gap
