Fork: how to ask "how thorough?"

The machinery has two real axes — search effort (rapid · standard · deep) and analysis depth (landscape · standard · deep). The interviews say users think in one thoroughness dial.

Option A — one question, three presets + customise (recommended)

Quick look = rapid search · landscape depth (~10 min) · Standard review = standard · standard (~20 min) · Deep analysis = deep · deep, findings extracted (~45 min). A quiet "customise" affordance exposes the two real axes for the users who want them (the real transcript's author picked rapid search + standard depth — a mix no preset covers, so customise isn't hypothetical). Free text always compiles any mix.

Option B — two questions (search, then analysis)

Honest to the machinery, and the only way every combination is button-reachable — but it's a fifth turn on the slow path, and the distinction ("effort of finding" vs "depth of reading") needs explaining every time. The interviews' overload finding argues against it.

Behaviour rules (binding on the build)

Free text beats buttons everywhere — parts are suggestions, not a form; any reply can answer several parts, redirect, or ignore the question asked (the planner re-plans its remaining parts accordingly). · Change re-opens one part — downstream confirmations survive unless the change invalidates them (the planner says which, and why). · No part, no card — a turn whose reply carries no structured part renders as plain prose (old transcripts render unchanged; rehydration restores part + confirmed state from the durable column). · Never re-ask what's answered — compound messages pre-confirm in bulk with Change affordances. · The plan card is the only start surface — details open by default pre-run (standing ruling), 409 fence and two-phase turn persistence untouched.
