What does the evidence say about upfront-cost barriers for low-income households?

Upfront cost is the most consistently reported barrier across the evidence base. Grant-eligible households still faced average out-of-pocket costs of £4,200 after subsidy [1], and uptake among the lowest income quintile stayed below 3% even where grants covered the majority of installation [2]. Two studies link this to inability to finance the residual cost rather than low awareness [2][3].
