What was searched

This scoping claims breadth, not exhaustiveness, and its breadth is bounded by the evidence scope you set. Everything below is what the run retrieved, read, set aside or left unread.

Evidence base

1,240 candidate documents retrieved from OpenAlex and Overton for the plan, 96 of them inherited from your evidence search and re-screened against this plan. 412 passed the title-and-abstract screen: 268 academic, 144 grey literature. 2005 to 2026, English language.

What was read, and how deeply

Screening and the longlist read titles and abstracts only; every abstract that passed was read for the interventions it names. The assessment read the full text of 38 documents, the ones the seven profiles rely on, capped at twelve per option. Six documents had no obtainable full text and support abstract-level claims only, labelled as such in the profiles. Every claim in the report carries the depth of what was read.

Set aside as outside the evidence scope

23 documents were retrieved and set aside under "OECD evidence only": their study settings are outside the OECD. They are counted here, never used as support, and never a reason to exclude an option. Listed under Set aside.

Not read under the cap

19 documents that mention an assessed option were not read: 11 on wage subsidies, 5 on caseworker support, 3 on the youth guarantee. They are listed by option; a full evidence search reads them and confirms the set.

Constraints applied

No benefit cuts or sanctions, on the options at the longlist: one excluded. OECD evidence only, on the documents at retrieval and screening: 23 set aside. Low cost per participant, on the assessed options: unresolved for five, breaks for one. Default screens: relevant to the outcomes · distinct · in scope.

Not searched

Live official statistics and departmental pages: the baseline reaches only what Overton and OpenAlex hold. The open web. No document found on the cost of careers guidance, or on the effect of apprenticeship incentives on the NEET rate.
