Youth guarantee

Open the full reportCompare with scoping passStart a theory of changeSwap on shortlist

Every 16 to 24 year old who leaves education or work gets a guaranteed offer of a job, apprenticeship, training or continued education within four months. It creates an entitlement, and puts the burden of supplying offers on local providers.

At a glance

  • How big, as reported (updated): fourteen of twenty-three studies found NEET rate reductions of one to three points. Two studies now report sustained education or employment four points higher at twelve months.
  • How sure (updated): moderate. Twenty-three studies, two with a regression-discontinuity design; five found no effect.
  • Where tried (updated): nine jurisdictions evaluated, adding Greece and Slovenia. All EU, none UK.
  • Transferability: still conditional. The evidence side is now cited across four studies, but two support factors stay unknown because your context has not changed.
  • Key assumption (updated): local providers can supply enough quality offers within four months. Still load-bearing; three implementation studies now quantify offer supply, so the evidence for it is moderate rather than weak.

What changed since the scoping pass

  • Documents. Sixty-one kept of eighty-four candidates after full-text confirmation. The scoping pass screened title and abstract only.
  • Studies. Nine, now twenty-three, quality-screened.
  • Claims. The mechanism and its failure mode are now cited rather than inferred. Three reasoning claims remain, and are labelled.
  • Counter-case. Stronger. Spain and Greece add two null findings; the twelve-month employment gap is partly filled.
  • Your context. Unchanged. Two support factors stay unknown until you state them in the plan.
