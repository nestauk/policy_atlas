Youth guarantee

Open the full reportCompare with scoping passStart a theory of changeRemove from shortlist

Every 16 to 24 year old who leaves education or work gets a guaranteed offer of a job, apprenticeship, training or continued education within four months. It creates an entitlement, and puts the burden of supplying offers on local providers.

At a glance

  • How big, as reported (updated): fourteen of twenty-three studies report NEET rate reductions of one to three points against the pre-guarantee trend, 2014 to 2023. Two studies report sustained education or employment four points higher at twelve months.
  • How sure (updated): moderate. Twenty-three independent studies from sixty-one documents, all read in full; five found no effect.
  • Where tried (updated): nine jurisdictions evaluated, adding Greece and Slovenia. All EU, none UK.
  • Transferability: still conditional on: local delivery funded. The report computed the verdict with your context passed in from this task, so the condition persists until you state the fact.
  • Key assumption (updated): local providers can supply enough quality offers within four months. Still load-bearing; three implementation studies now quantify offer supply, so the evidence for it is moderate rather than weak.

What changed since the scoping pass

  • Documents. Sixty-one confirmed in full text of eighty-four candidates. The scoping pass read seven in full and confirmed none.
  • Studies. Nine documents, now twenty-three independent studies: independence is known once full text is read.
  • Judgement cells. Computed by the report with your context: how sure and the key assumption changed; the transferability verdict and its condition did not.
  • Counter-case. Stronger. Spain and Greece add two null findings; the twelve-month employment gap is partly filled.
  • Your context. Unchanged. Funded local delivery is still a plan, not a fact.
