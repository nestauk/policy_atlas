Sense-check: a youth guarantee for 16 to 24 year olds who become NEET

ExportOpen the full profileWiden: explore the option space

Top line

Collapse −

The guarantee depends on local providers being able to supply enough suitable offers. The documents read report NEET rate reductions among registered young people; they do not establish that the same would hold for the inactive group in the UK. Evidence and assumptions.

First clarify funding, the intended population and the take-up condition. These determine which design is being considered. This is Policy Atlas's reasoning from the evidence and the missing UK context.

Rapid · 1 option assessed · full text read for 4 of the 9 mentioning documents · 5 not read under the cap · neighbours not assessed. Effects are stated against their own comparators and periods; nothing is compared with the UK baseline, which this scoping does not forecast.

Questions to put to the department

Collapse −
  • Would local delivery be funded, and through which route? The transferability verdict rests on it.
  • Is a take-up condition intended? Every evaluated guarantee had one; a sanction-free design has no evidence yet.
  • Which group is the guarantee for: everyone who becomes NEET, or the inactive group driving the rise, whom guarantees reached least?
  • What offer-quality floor is intended? Results were weakest where none was set.

Unknowns only; none carries a recommendation.

The problem and what is contested

Expand +

About one in eight 16 to 24 year olds is NEET, rising since 2022, the rise in inactivity; sources disagree about why.

Youth guarantee

Collapse −

Every 16 to 24 year old who becomes NEET gets a guaranteed offer of a job, apprenticeship, training or education within four months. Provide a service, touching regulate; structural, as described. Most implementations attach a benefit sanction to taking up the offer.

  • How big, as reported: four of the four documents read report NEET rate reductions of one to three points against the pre-guarantee trend, for 15 to 24 year olds registering as NEET, 2014 to 2021. Five documents were not read under the rapid cap.
  • How sure: moderate, on four documents read in full. Five more mention it, unread.
  • Where tried: EU-wide since 2013; Finland, Austria, Italy and Spain in the documents read.
  • Transferability: conditional on: local delivery funded. The weakest leg decides; nothing retrieved or stated settles it.
  • Key assumption, test first: local providers can supply enough quality offers within four months. Load-bearing; the evidence for it is weak.
