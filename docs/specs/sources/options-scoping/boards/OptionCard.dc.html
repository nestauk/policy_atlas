Youth guarantee

Remove from shortlistExclude

A guaranteed offer of a job, apprenticeship, training or education within four months of becoming NEET.

What it is for

NEET rate and time spent NEET, two of the three outcomes in your plan. It works on local providers first, and reaches young people through them.

What the evidence base holds so far

Nine studies from seven countries: three academic and six grey literature, the earliest from 2016. Screened on title and abstract only. Nothing has been read in full yet, so there is no assessment of how big or how sure.

Screens and guesses

Breaks "No benefit cuts or sanctions", because most implementations attach a sanction to the offer. It stays because you added it to the shortlist, with your note to assess it without sanctions. Cost: likely high. That is a guess from reasoning, not from evidence; the assessment will replace it with reported costs.
