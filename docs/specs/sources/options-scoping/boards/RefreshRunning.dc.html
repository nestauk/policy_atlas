Top line

Caseworker support and wage subsidies have the strongest evidence in this reading set, and their populations differ from the group driving the rise. The caseworker findings concern the long-term unemployed; the wage-subsidy findings concern the job-ready. See the option verdicts.

A youth guarantee stays conditional on funded local delivery. No matching implementation was found for the sanction-free variant. Inspect the profile.

The next decision is whether to keep the broad target or focus further work on economically inactive young people. This is Policy Atlas's reasoning from the population mismatch, not a measured effect. Reported costs do not support a common cost comparison.

7 options reviewed at standard depth · 5 with outcome evidence · 1 with implementation evidence only · 1 where assessment was attempted and no matching evidence was found. Seven other included options were not assessed; one was excluded. Each effect is stated against its own comparator, population and period; the options are not compared with each other or with the UK baseline, which this scoping does not forecast.
