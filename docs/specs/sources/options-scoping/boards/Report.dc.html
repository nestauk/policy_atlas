Youth guarantee

What it is

A youth guarantee commits the state to offer every young person who becomes NEET a job, apprenticeship, traineeship or continued education within a fixed period, usually four months [1][2]. It was adopted EU-wide in 2013 and has been evaluated in nine member states [3]. In every evaluated case the guarantee was built on an existing youth employment service rather than replacing it [3][7]. cited · 4 sources

How it works

Registration starts a clock. The deadline converts a search obligation on the young person into a supply obligation on providers, which forces early contact [4][9]. Three implementation studies find that offer supply, not registration, is the binding constraint in the first two years [9][12][15]. cited · 5 sources

Main failure mode. Where offers are short training courses with no quality floor, young people return to NEET within months and the headline rate improves while time spent NEET does not [6][11]. Italy and Spain show this pattern over five years [6][8]. cited · 4 sources

Whether a guarantee can run without any sanction attached to the offer is not tested in the evaluated cases; every scheme paired the entitlement with at least a registration duty. reasoning, not evidence · labelled
